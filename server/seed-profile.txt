I'm a New Zealander interested in substantive news and analysis.

Topics I care about most:
- New Zealand politics, policy, and public affairs — especially anything affecting everyday life, housing, healthcare, or the economy
- Technology, particularly AI developments, software industry news, and implications for society and work
- Climate change: science, policy, and real-world impact — especially in the Pacific and Australasia
- World affairs with geopolitical depth — not just breaking news but analysis of why things are happening
- Business and economics, particularly NZ's economic conditions, interest rates, and housing market

Sources I trust and enjoy: RNZ, Newsroom, The Guardian, BBC. I prefer long-form and analytical pieces over quick news briefs. I like pieces that give context, not just facts.

Topics I don't need much of:
- Sport (occasional interest but not daily)
- Celebrity, lifestyle, and entertainment
- Crime stories without wider social context
- Opinion pieces that are more provocative than substantive
- Royals, lottery results, or social media trends
- Generic business wire stories with no NZ relevance
